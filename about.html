About

Designer by eye,
developer by hand

I'm Hakim Waithaka Castro — a creative designer and developer, originally from Kenya.

My work lives where the two crafts overlap: I design the idea and then build it myself, so nothing gets lost between the mockup and the launch. The result is work that feels considered at every layer — the concept, the pixels, and the code underneath.

Growing up in Kenya taught me to make bold things with what's at hand — that resourcefulness still shapes how I approach every project: strong ideas first, clean execution always.
